# The archive desk's queue — what the history database grows by next

The weekly archive desk takes the first three unchecked items, researches them under CONTRACT.md (bilingual, sourced, Commons-only free
pictures, never an invented URL), appends rows to the files of data/research/v35/, ticks them here with the date, regenerates, checks,
commits and pushes. Owners add items at the end or reorder; the desk never deletes rows.
