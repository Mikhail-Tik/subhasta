Subhasta, from the Latin sub hasta: in Rome, goods were auctioned beneath a spear planted in the ground, the praeco called the bids and the argentarius settled the money. Figures here are as reported by the houses, Arts Economics (Art Basel & UBS 2026), ArtTactic, Bank of America Private Bank, Artprice and the trade press; every dossier, lot and signal links its sources, or says that it does not yet (the flag U). Nothing here is investment, legal or tax advice: every figure is a reading of the public record, and a professional’s number ends every route. Provenance records come from the National Gallery of Art’s open data (CC0). Prices include buyer’s premium; private-treaty prices are press reports.
